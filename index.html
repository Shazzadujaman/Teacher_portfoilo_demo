About Me

I'm Prof. Dr. A K M Motinur Rahman

Vice-Chancellor & Professor – Dept. of Public Administration

Professor Dr. A K M Motinur Rahman is the Vice-Chancellor of Islamic University, Kushtia, Bangladesh, and a distinguished Professor in the Department of Public Administration. He holds a Doctoral Degree (Ph.D.) in Development and NGO Operations from Islamic University, an M.A. in International Relations from the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Sydney, Australia, and B.S.S (Hon's) & M.S.S in Political Science from the University of Dhaka (First Class 3rd). With over 28 years of academic teaching, high-level university administration, and international research expertise, he has served as Proctor, Hall Provost, Press Administrator, Department Chairman, and Director of the Centre for Social Studies. He was also elected President of the Islamic University Teachers' Association and Vice-President of the Federation of Bangladesh University Teachers' Association.
